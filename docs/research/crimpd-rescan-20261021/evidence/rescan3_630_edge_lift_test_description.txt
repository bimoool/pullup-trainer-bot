This testing session is designed to assess finger strength using a single-arm edge lifting protocol using an edge size of approximately 20mm. 

You should use this session before and after several weeks or months of training to measure the effectiveness of your training. Results from this session also provide an excellent baseline from which to calculate the intensities of other sessions, such as max lifts. 

Complete this session after at least 24 hours of rest from any other climbing or training. Remember to warm up thoroughly before starting this session and warm up your fingers specifically for the hold size, grip position, and body position. 

Complete up to 8 sets of progressively harder lifts until you reach a maximal score. Each lift must follow the rules below:

-  Complete each lift using a strict half crimp position
-  Each lift must be 7 seconds long
-  If you are unable to maintain a strict half crimp position for all 7 seconds the lift is considered failed
-  At the completion of the each rep, rest 30 sec and then repeat the test with the other arm

A max score is the highest load completed for the set time staying in the same grip position throughout. Once the load is too high for you to maintain either the grip or lift, cease the test and record your scores. 

Recording your score:

Maximal total loading (1RM) = Total load lifted

Safety guidance:

Any maximal testing session includes some form of risk. Please follow the rules below to reduce the risk of injury and always seek advice from a professional coach if you are unsure of how to perform this testing safely. 

-  Do not change grip positions whilst lifting. If your fingers move or open out whilst lifting, you should stop immediately. 
-  Load your fingers cautiously and progressively when building up to your maximum during testing. 
-  Increase the load of each set progressively, never exceeding an adjustment of more than 5kg on any set. Typically adjustments of 1-2kg are normal. 
-  If you have not reached a max load within 8 sets, finish testing and complete this session on another day.
